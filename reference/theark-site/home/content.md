— THE LAND

## From overgrazed pasture to living ecosystem

This land has a story. For decades it was used for cattle grazing — until it was depleted, left with barely 15 plant species and little productive soil remaining.

Marat discovered it by chance in 2021, over breakfast in Santa Teresa. He saw past what it was to what it could become. By 2022 The ARK was born.

The early years were hard. No sales, no outside funding — just conviction. That period became the foundation. The team built the permaculture farm from scratch. Today the land supports over 400 plant species and a growing community.

One decision defines what The ARK stands for more than any other: Joaquín Sánchez's family — stewards of this land since 1966 — were offered 25% of all proceeds from The ARK's businesses. Not as a transaction. As a recognition that this land has a history worth honoring.

87 residential lots, each a minimum of 5,000m². Fewer lots by design, so more land stays wild, restored, and regenerating.
