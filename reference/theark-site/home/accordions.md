### How do I get to The ARK?

The ARK is located in Santa Teresa, Costa Rica — 5 minutes from the beach on the Nicoya Peninsula.

• Closest airport: Cobano — 30-minute flight from San José (SJO)

• San José (SJO) — approximately 1.5–2 hours by road and ferry

• Liberia (LIR) — approximately 2 hours by road

Direct flights into Cobano operate from San José daily.

### Is there fast internet for remote work?

### Can I bring my pets?

Yes — dogs and cats are welcome at The ARK. Costa Rica has no quarantine period for pets arriving with the correct paperwork, making the process more straightforward than most countries.

What you'll need:

• Health certificate — issued by a licensed vet within 15 days of travel, confirming your pet is healthy and free of parasites

• Vaccinations — rabies is required for all cats and dogs over 3 months, administered at least 21 days before travel. Dogs also need distemper, hepatitis, parvovirus and leptospirosis

• Parasite treatment — internal and external parasite treatment administered by a vet within 15 days of departure

• Travel options — pets can travel in-cabin (under 15 lbs), as checked baggage, or as cargo. No import permit is needed if your pet travels with you on the same flight

Note: Birds, reptiles, and exotic animals require additional permits and are considered on a case-by-case basis by Costa Rican authorities. We recommend consulting SENASA — Costa Rica's National Animal Health Service — or a pet relocation specialist for the most current requirements before you travel.

Once you're here, Santa Teresa is one of the most pet-friendly communities in Costa Rica. Your pets will love it as much as you will.

### How does land ownership work as a foreigner?

Costa Rica allows foreigners to hold titled property with the same rights as citizens. You receive a full freehold title in your name. We have an established legal framework and walk every buyer through the process.

### Can I visit before committing to buy?

Absolutely — and we encourage it. Book a guest stay, attend an event, or join a founder webinar. Many of our residents visited once before deciding.

### What if I want to sell my lot later?

You own the land outright and can sell at any time at market value. The ARK team assists with resale. Fractional shares are also resellable at any time.

### What healthcare is available nearby?

Local clinics in Santa Teresa and Cobano. The ARK Holistic Health Center opens on-site in 2027. San José has world-class private hospitals accessible within 1.5 hours.
