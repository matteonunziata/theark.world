WHAT IS FRACTIONAL OWNERSHIP
## Become a
Seasonal Steward.

Fractional ownership lets you own a share of a fully finished private villa on a 5,000m² estate within The ARK. Eight individual homes sit on the lot — each beautifully designed and built to the ARK's architectural standards. You own your share. You enjoy your weeks. If you choose to, the property earns for you.

#### Immediate ROI

You earn a return from the property from the first year of operations — without building, managing, or maintaining anything yourself.

#### Profit sharing from all ARK businesses

As a shareholder you participate in the village-wide profit share — from events and hospitality to the farm and real estate sales.

#### Fully managed

The ARK team handles everything — rental management, guest relations, maintenance, and property care — at 20% commission.

#### Resellable at any time

Shares can be resold at any time based on the current market value of the property.
